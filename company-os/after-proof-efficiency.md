## Five instruments

1. **CAC payback (gross-margin adjusted).** Months to recover acquisition cost on gross profit, not revenue. Unadjusted payback is fiction when margin is thin.

2. **NRR and GRR.** Net and gross revenue retention on the paid base. Pilots fake NRR — a concierge or discounted pilot that “renews” is not retention.

3. **Usage, not seats.** Price the job done. Seat counts compress. [Benchmarkit 2026](https://www.benchmarkit.ai/2026-saas-ai-native-metrics): usage-based kept NRR above seat-based. That is why this instrument exists, not a cutoff we invented.

4. **Gross margin.** The adjustment on payback and magic number. Ignore it and the other four lie.

5. **Magic number only if margin-adjusted.** `(net new ARR × gross margin) / prior-quarter S&M`. Dated stop/may, [Benchmarkit 2026](https://www.benchmarkit.ai/2026-saas-ai-native-metrics) until replaced: **0.75 stop-spend** / **1.0 may-spend**. Do not invent other cutoffs.
