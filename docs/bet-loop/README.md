# Company bet loop

A bet is a claim that a target customer will take a costly step. Test the claim before building on it.

Synthetic testing comes first, and it is pre-registered. A pass then pairs with one real-world check. Real-world work runs one bet at a time, in order: smoke, organic, warm list, workshop. Each tier lasts at most two weeks.

Roadmap changes start from the bet list. A study verdict does not move the roadmap until Cos signs it.

Copy this folder into the company repo and fill the blanks below. The rules stay. The blanks are the company.

## Fill in for this company

Four fields define the loop. Three more lines fence it.

### Company line

What you sell, and to whom.

> _Fill in one sentence._

### Target customers

Who the best customer is. What they do when they find you. Who you are not for.

> _Fill in._

### Costly step

The action that costs the customer real money, time, or reputation. Stated liking does not count. Each costly step needs the founder's yes before it runs. The rule is in the [validation workflow](validation-workflow.md#costly-steps).

> _Name the step._

### Blocked list

What a test must never do or say. The working list is [blocked-list.md](blocked-list.md). Add company rows there.

### Channels

Where customers find you today.

> _Fill in._

### Channel fence

Which of those channels a test may touch. Record it in the [validation workflow](validation-workflow.md#channel-fence). A channel left off the fence is off limits.

### Budget and stop rule

The monthly test budget and the time limit. Record both in the [validation workflow](validation-workflow.md#budget-and-stop-rule). The designer cost cap is in [study standards](study-standards.md#designer-cost-cap).

## Roles

Shared across companies. One copy of the role serves many companies:

- Designer
- Tester Synthetic
- Tester Real World

Per repo. Each product repo has its own:

- Builder

Support roles:

- Drafter: writes posts and outreach. A human approves each send.
- Board mirror: reflects tier status. Read-only. Never edits the repo.

Shared is the default. A company may run its own Designer or Testers when privacy or volume calls for it. See [roles.md](roles.md#shared-by-default-own-copies-when-needed).

Cos arbitrates for the company. The CTO design gate is the check before build. Both are in [roles.md](roles.md).
