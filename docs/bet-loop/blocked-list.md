# Blocked list

A test must never do or say anything in this table. The designer cites the specific row in every variant's blocked-list check. A variant with no citation is not ready.

Add a row when onboarding names a forbidden move, or when a killed bet shows a move that burns money, time, or reputation. Put the date and the origin in Source: an onboarding answer, a killed-bet id, or a gate decision.

Write the behavior that is blocked. Do not write customer names or other personal details.

The starter rows below are generic. Keep them, tighten them, or extend them. Do not delete a starter row until a gate decision says why.

| Blocked | Why | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bought weight or influence | Paying for rank, reviews, followers, or introductions, and treating that as proof. It is not a target customer taking a costly step. | Template starter |
| Unverified one-sided marks | A score, badge, or endorsement that only one party asserts, with no check. | Template starter |
| A new identifier without a gate decision | A new id, handle, or account issued before the CTO design gate records a dated scope. | Template starter |

Company rows go under the starter rows, in the same three columns.
