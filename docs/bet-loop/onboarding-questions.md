# Onboarding questions

Bootstrap Bill asks these in order. Write each answer under the heading named here. One fact lives in one place. Question 11 is optional.

| # | Question | Where the answer goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What you sell and to whom | [Company line](README.md#company-line) |
| 2 | Best customer, what they do when they find you, who you are not for | [Target customers](README.md#target-customers) |
| 3 | The one thing to prove in 30 days | [First bet](bet-list-format.md#first-bet) |
| 4 | What you tried that failed, and why | [Past bets (killed)](bet-list-format.md#past-bets-killed), and candidate rows on the [blocked list](blocked-list.md) |
| 5 | Which steps cost real money, time, or reputation | [Costly step](README.md#costly-step), and [costly steps](validation-workflow.md#costly-steps) (each needs the founder's yes) |
| 6 | What a test must never do or say | [Blocked list](blocked-list.md) |
| 7 | Where customers find you | [Channels](README.md#channels) |
| 8 | Which channels a test may touch | [Channel fence](validation-workflow.md#channel-fence) |
| 9 | Monthly test budget and time limit | [Budget and stop rule](validation-workflow.md#budget-and-stop-rule), and the [designer cost cap](study-standards.md#designer-cost-cap) |
| 10 | What makes you stop, keep going, or change course | [Advance, iterate, or kill](validation-workflow.md#advance-iterate-or-kill) |
| 11 (optional) | Who sees results, and where | [Cos report subscribers](roles.md#cos-report-subscribers) |

Bill stops when an answer is missing, so the founder can fill that blank. Bill does not invent a second home for the same fact.
