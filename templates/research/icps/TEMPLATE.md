Copy this file once per candidate (`icp-01.md`, `icp-02.md`, …).  
Need **at least three** before you treat research as started.  
Rank is test priority. **Promote = hold** until the promotion rule is met.


Promotion (from the OS): weigh **stated**, **synthetic**, and **observed**. Stated is real input — especially present pain and “no” reasons — and a weak predictor of a future buy. Synthetic work may **rank or kill**. When stated and observed disagree, **observed** wins. Promote stays **hold** until synthetic **and** observed both exist, reward looks real, and risks look manageable for a solo founder **at this stage**. Improving scores on a weak group is less valuable than finding a stronger one.
