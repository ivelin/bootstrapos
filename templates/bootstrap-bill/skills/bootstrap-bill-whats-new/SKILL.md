Keep a short plain-language what's-new for the current packed version. Never teach "webhook," MCP URL paste, or subscribe_board in founder-facing copy. The public Bill page and Install Bootstrap OS links are required in founder onboarding.

Current highlights (Nov 2026):

- New (Nov 2026): Bill helps you onboard the pirin.ai portfolio bots from the members shelf (CTO, builder, code reviewer, site builder, product manager, general manager, content and research). It recommends the one or two that fit your bottleneck, preps each bot's first chat with you, shows you how to get the CTO, builder and reviewer working together, and checks in after a week. Your logins and keys stay with the bot that needs them, never with Bill.
- New (Nov 2026): a weekly checkpoint on your bet board. Once a week Bill drafts one short comment for your bottleneck and every live bet: does the bottleneck still hold or has it been met, does each bet have a done-check, a target date and a kill rule, and what moved this week. Bill posts it only after you say yes.
- New (Nov 2026): a post-mortem for everything you retire. Once a month Bill writes a thorough look back for each retired bottleneck or bet: what worked, what failed, what we learned and what we improve next time. A met bottleneck gets retired within 7 days, so it never sits stale on the card.
- New (Nov 2026): Bill records how each founder joined (warm invite, workshop, self-serve, seeded from a signed agreement, other) as a dated note on the board, so you can tell which bets actually brought people in.
- Board cards match the standard format out of the box: a title line on what you sell and to whom, 🎯 on the one thing that matters most, an icon on every row, a legend and a **Next:** line. Ask Bill if you'd like it different.
- Before connecting, Bill tells you exactly what it will share with Bootstrap OS and waits for your OK.
- Bill connects Bootstrap OS for you at setup, before anything else.
- Bill catches itself going in circles and asks you for new evidence instead.
- Every check Bill runs covers all your companies the same way; nothing is wired to one company or one bet.
- Feedback to bootstrap@pirin.ai. When you're stuck or something feels off, Bill offers to put together a comprehensive feedback report for you to send.
- Getting in (Nov 10, 2026): every new user is pointed to https://www.pirin.ai/bots/bootstrap-bill. Create a free pirin.ai account, open that page and sign in with your own login, follow Install Bootstrap OS, then connect Bootstrap OS with that same account.

When briefing an upgrade: what changed, what to reconnect, what stayed the same, offer onboarding again. No PII.
