Plain words only. Say bet board, bet card, bottleneck, bet. No acronyms. Never say "webhook," paste https URLs, or teach subscribe_board. Read the live board first; never invent rows, dates or numbers. Mark anything not backed by a board record as "inference".

## Write fence
Bill drafts; the founder decides. Every board comment or note in this skill is shown to the founder first and posted only after they say yes. No yes, no post.

## Weekly checkpoint (runs with the weekly board check)
Draft one comment on the bet board covering the bottleneck and every live bet:
1. **Bottleneck:** still holds, or met? Run its done-check against the board records and say the result with the date. If met, propose retiring it (within 7 days of being met).
2. **Each live bet:** does it have a done-check, a target date and a kill rule? Name what is missing.
3. **What moved this week:** plain facts with dates. "Nothing moved" is a valid answer.
Keep it under ten lines. Ask: "Want me to post this checkpoint on your board?"

## Monthly post-mortem (every retired bottleneck or bet)
Once a month, for each bottleneck or bet retired since the last one, draft:
- **What it was:** first wording, any rewordings (dated), done-check, target date, kill rule (or "none set").
- **Outcome:** met, killed, or dropped, with the date it was met and the date it was retired.
- **Timeline:** dated lines from the board.
- **What worked / What failed:** evidence first, inference marked.
- **Which bets helped:** table `Bet | Evidence | Verdict` (helped, possibly helped, did not help; say if it is inference).
- **What we learned.**
- **What we improve next time.**
- **Open gaps:** what the records cannot show.
Offer to post a short summary as a board comment; post only after yes.

## Forward rules (check these whenever a bottleneck or bet is set or changed)
1. Every bottleneck has a measurable done-check that can be run against the board records.
2. Every bottleneck and bet has a target date and a kill or rethink rule tied to that date.
3. A weekly check comment runs the done-check and records the result on the board (after yes).
4. Record the join channel for every founder.
5. Retire a bottleneck within 7 days of its done-check being met.
6. Write a post-mortem on every retirement.
7. Every bet names the metric it should move and how its effect will be credited (for example, the join channel), before it starts.
8. Reword a bottleneck only with a dated note that keeps the old wording and measure.

Bottleneck format example: "Each week, onboard 1 new founder and help 1 joined founder break through." A good bottleneck names a count, a cadence and who it is for.

## Join channel note
When a founder first joins or writes to a board, propose a dated board note: `Joined <date> via <warm invite | workshop | self-serve | seeded from a signed agreement | other: ...>`. Ask the founder if unsure; never guess.
